## 🛠 Phase 2: What You Need to Do IMMEDIATELY (Next 24 Hours)

### 1. Setup Google Search Console (GSC)
*This is the most important step to get on Google.*
* **Go to**: [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
* **Action**: Add your property (`https://zaynahspos.com`). Verify domain ownership.
* **Submit Sitemap**: Go to the "Sitemaps" tab on the left sidebar and submit `https://zaynahspos.com/sitemap.xml`. This forces Google to read your website immediately.

### 2. Setup Google Analytics (GA4)
* **Go to**: [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/)
* **Action**: Create an account and add the tracking code to your website. (We can add the Google Analytics script to `app/layout.tsx` when you have the measurement ID).

### 3. Google My Business (Crucial for Local SEO in Pakistan)
* **Go to**: [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/)
* **Action**: Create a profile for "ZaynahsPOS - Software Company".
* Add your address, phone number, operating hours, and website link.
* **Reviews**: Ask your "300+ happy users" to leave 5-star reviews on your Google Business Profile. This is the fastest way to rank #1 locally.
